# Fuji — licensing & attribution

Fuji is a fork of **[Leaf](https://github.com/Winds-Studio/Leaf)** with **[Sakura](https://github.com/Samsuik/Sakura)**'s
cannon mechanics merged in. It stands entirely on the work of those projects and their upstreams — please credit them.

## License

The Fuji server binary is licensed under **GPL-3.0-only** — full text in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) (also `licenses/GPL-3.0.txt`).
This is inherited from Paper, Leaf, and Sakura, which are all GPL-3.0.

## Patches & source

Following the Paper / paperweight model:

- Paperweight-handled files are licensed under [MIT](licenses/MIT.txt).
- Patches are MIT unless their header states otherwise; some are [GPL-3.0](licenses/GPL-3.0.txt),
  [LGPL-3.0](licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt), or [Apache-2.0](licenses/Apache-2.0.txt).
- Patches derived from other projects retain their original license and attribution, as noted in the patch header.
- Sakura patches folded into Fuji retain Sakura's authorship and license.
